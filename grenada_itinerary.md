> **Lessons baked in from the real trip:**
> - **BBC / Morne Rouge anchorage** is far less rolly than the Grand Anse mooring balls — use it as start/end base.
> - **Ronde Island** was the surprise star — remote anchorage, black-sand beach, great snorkeling. Hit twice (north + south bound).
> - **Bathway Beach has NO charted anchorage** — removed from route. Black Bay is a passing stop only.
> - Route now runs **island-to-island up the middle** (Ronde → Carriacou → Union → Mayreau → Tobago Cays), not hugging Grenada's west coast.

## Day-by-Day

**Day 1 — Arrival · BBC / Morne Rouge base.** Aboard by early afternoon. Anchor BBC/Morne Rouge (calmer than Grand Anse moorings). Afternoon dinghy loop: BBC · Portici · Magazine beaches + beachfront hotel bars for sundowners. *Night 1: BBC.*

**Day 2 — Early run north to Ronde Island.** ~6am departure. Black Bay breakfast/lunch stop underway. On to Ronde Island — remote anchorage, black-sand beach, remote snorkeling. *Night 2: Ronde Island.*

**Day 3 — Carriacou · clear out of Grenada.** Sail to Carriacou; clear OUT of Grenada (Hillsborough/Tyrrel Bay). Paradise Beach (L'Esterre Bay) — anchor or mooring ball. *Night 3: Paradise Beach, Carriacou.*

**Day 4 — Union Island · clear into SVG → Chatham Bay.** Sail to Union; make landfall at **Clifton Harbour** and **clear INTO SVG** there first (30–45 min). Once cleared, reposition around to **Chatham Bay** on Union's west side (big, protected, undeveloped; beach-shack dinners) for the night. *Night 4: Chatham Bay, Union.*

**Day 5 — Mayreau.** Short sail; anchor **Carenage Bay**. Dinner ashore at the **Windward Mayreau resort restaurant** — walk out, cab back. *Night 5: Mayreau.*

**Day 6 — Southern Grenadines pick.** Choose by weather + mood: Tobago Cays (turtles/reef), Palm Island (polished private island), or Petite Martinique (quiet/local — note: this is Grenada waters). *Night 6: chosen spot.*

**★ FLEX day/night — floating.** One night not nailed down. Sean drops it where needed: weather hold, or bank an extra night at a favorite. Default if unused: 2nd night at Tobago Cays.

**Day 7 — South to Ronde Island.** Sail south, timed to reach Ronde for the night — second helping of the best remote anchorage on the route. *Night 7: Ronde Island.*

**Day 8 — Back to base.** Early sail to BBC/Grand Anse. Final beach afternoon, pack, last sundowners. *Night 8: BBC/Grand Anse.*

**Day 9 — Departure.** Slow morning, final swim. Option: pay to depart from a marina (confirm which with Sean), or add a night for a true 9-night trip.
